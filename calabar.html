BEYOND THE PARADE

The two-attraction
experience.

Take your journey beyond the Carnival with two distinctive experiences in Cross River.

DESTINATION 01

Tinapa Free Zone & Resort

A structured leisure and entertainment stop north of Calabar. Depending on operating status on your selected date, the experience may include resort scenery, leisure facilities, food, retail and photo opportunities.

DESTINATION 02

Kwa Falls

A green nature escape inside the Cross River National Park area. Expect a scenic drive, nature, photography and an active walk to the falls. The approach includes a substantial stair descent and uneven terrain.

Our attraction promise

The standard Explore route includes exactly two attractions: Tinapa and Kwa Falls. Access, opening status, road conditions, guide arrangements, weather and applicable entrance fees are reconfirmed before departure.

GOOD TO KNOW

Your questions,
answered.

Helpful information before you plan your Calabar experience.

What is included in Seat Only?

A hospitality seat, event-day meal, bottled water, non-alcoholic refreshment, check-in, seat guidance and event-day WhatsApp support. Hotels, flights, airport pickup and attractions are excluded.

How much is the Seat Only package?

₦150,000 per person, subject to confirmed hospitality allocation.

Can I add accommodation after buying my seat?

Yes, subject to hotel availability. A new package quotation will be calculated for your dates.

Which attractions are included?

Tinapa Free Zone & Resort and Kwa Falls. These are the two standard Explore attractions.

How long is the tour package?

Approximately three to five days, with two, three or four hotel nights depending on the itinerary.

Does every package include flights?

No. Seat, Stay and Explore exclude flights. Fly & Explore includes a return flight subject to origin, date, fare and availability.

Are airport transfers included?

Where specified in Fly & Explore and hosted international packages. Domestic Stay and Explore guests can add transfers.

Will my hotel be named before payment?

A hotel class may initially be displayed. Named hotel and room confirmation are subject to supplier availability and terms.

Can I book for a group?

Yes. Couples, families, associations, companies and diaspora groups can request coordinated rooms, seating and transport.

Is Davido confirmed to perform?

His proposed star-artist billing remains subject to formal event confirmation and approval.

What happens after I book?

After a genuine booking and payment are confirmed, guests should receive a booking reference, itinerary, pickup details, attraction schedule, event guidance and support contacts.

Are prices refundable?

Cancellation and refunds depend on the relevant hospitality, hotel, flight and transport supplier terms. Full conditions must be confirmed before payment.
