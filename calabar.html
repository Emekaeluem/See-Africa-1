GOOD TO KNOW

Your questions,
answered.

Useful details before planning your Calabar experience.

What is included in Seat Only?

A verified hospitality seat, event-day meal, bottled water, non-alcoholic refreshment, guest check-in, seat guidance and event-day WhatsApp support. Hotels, airport pickup, flights and attractions are excluded.

How much is Seat Only?

₦150,000 per person, subject to confirmation of the hospitality allocation.

Can I add accommodation after buying a seat?

Yes, subject to availability. Your package will be recalculated for your selected hotel and dates.

Which attractions are included?

The standard Explore route includes exactly two attractions: Tinapa Free Zone & Resort and Kwa Falls.

How long is the Explore package?

Domestic Explore packages are designed for approximately three to five days, with two, three or four hotel nights depending on the itinerary.

Does every package include flights?

No. Seat, Stay and Explore exclude flights. Fly & Explore is flight-inclusive, with final airfare subject to origin, route, dates and availability.

Are airport transfers included?

They are included where specifically stated in Fly & Explore and hosted international packages. Domestic Stay and Explore guests may add a transfer.

Is my hotel named before payment?

A hotel class may be displayed initially. The specific hotel and room arrangements must be confirmed according to supplier availability and terms.

Can I book for a group?

Yes. Couples, families, companies, associations and diaspora groups can request coordinated seats, rooms and transportation.

Is Davido confirmed to perform?

Davido's star-artist billing is subject to formal event confirmation and approved publicity materials.

What happens after I book?

After a real reservation and payment are confirmed, guests should receive a booking reference, itinerary, pickup details, guest instructions, attraction schedule and support contacts.

Are prices refundable?

Refunds depend on the applicable hospitality, hotel, airline and transport supplier terms. Conditions and deadlines must be provided before payment.
