GOOD TO KNOW

Your questions,
answered.

Everything you need to understand before planning your Carnival experience.

What is included in Seat Only?

One verified hospitality seat, an event-day meal, bottled water, non-alcoholic refreshments, check-in, seating guidance and event-day guest support. Accommodation, airport pickup, flights and attractions are excluded.

How much is Seat Only?

₦150,000 per person, subject to confirmed event-day allocation.

Can I add accommodation later?

Yes, subject to hotel availability and recalculated package pricing.

Which attractions are included?

The standard Explore route includes Tinapa Free Zone & Resort and Kwa Falls.

How long is the Explore package?

Approximately three to five days, with a choice of two, three or four hotel nights depending on your itinerary.

Does every package include flights?

No. Seat, Stay and Explore do not automatically include flights. Fly & Explore includes a return flight subject to actual origin, date and supplier fare.

Are airport transfers included?

They are included where specifically stated in a Fly & Explore or hosted international quotation. Domestic Stay and Explore guests can request additional transfers.

Will my hotel be named before payment?

A hotel class may be shown initially. A named hotel must be confirmed according to supplier availability and terms before payment.

Can I book for a group?

Yes. Couples, families, companies, associations and diaspora groups can request coordinated seating, accommodation and transport.

Is Davido confirmed to perform?

His proposed star-artist billing remains subject to formal event confirmation and approved promotional assets.

What happens after booking?

Once a genuine booking and payment are confirmed, guests should receive their booking reference, itinerary, instructions, pickup arrangements and contact details.

Are prices refundable?

Refunds depend on the applicable event, hotel, airline and transport supplier terms. Cancellation conditions must be confirmed before payment.
