GOOD TO KNOW

Your questions,
answered.

Helpful information before planning your Calabar experience.

What is included in Seat Only?

One verified hospitality seat, meal, bottled water, non-alcoholic refreshment, check-in, seat guidance and event-day WhatsApp support. Accommodation, flights, transfers and attractions are excluded.

How much is Seat Only?

₦150,000 per person, subject to confirmed hospitality allocation.

Can I add accommodation after buying my seat?

Yes, subject to availability. A revised package quotation will be provided for your dates.

Which attractions are included?

Tinapa Free Zone & Resort and Kwa Falls — exactly two attractions on the standard Explore itinerary.

How long is the Explore package?

Approximately three to five days, with two, three or four hotel nights depending on your itinerary.

Does every package include flights?

No. Seat, Stay and Explore exclude flights. Fly & Explore includes a return flight, subject to route, travel dates and fare confirmation.

Are airport transfers included?

Where specified in Fly & Explore and hosted international packages. Domestic Stay and Explore guests can add a shared or private transfer.

Will my hotel be named before payment?

Selected hotel class may be displayed until a room block is confirmed. A named hotel is subject to supplier availability and terms.

Can I book for a group?

Yes. Couples, families, organisations and diaspora groups can request coordinated seating, rooms, transport and guest support.

Is Davido confirmed to perform?

His star-artist listing remains subject to final event billing and formal approval. No live performance is guaranteed here.

What happens after I book?

After a real booking is accepted and payment confirmed, guests should receive a booking reference, itinerary, guest instructions, pickup details, attraction schedule and event guidance.

Are prices refundable?

Refunds depend on applicable seat, hotel, flight and transport supplier terms. Deposit, deadlines, cancellation conditions and penalties must be confirmed before payment.
