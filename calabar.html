BEYOND THE PARADE

The two-attraction
experience.

Extend your trip with two distinctive destinations in Cross River.

DESTINATION 01

Tinapa Free Zone & Resort

A structured leisure and entertainment stop north of Calabar. Depending on operating status on your selected date, the experience may include resort scenery, leisure facilities, food, retail and photo opportunities.

DESTINATION 02

Kwa Falls

A green nature escape inside the Cross River National Park area. Expect a scenic drive, photography and an active walk. The approach includes substantial stairs and uneven terrain.

Attraction promise

The standard Explore route includes exactly two attractions: Tinapa and Kwa Falls. Access, opening status, roads, weather, entrance fees and guide arrangements are reconfirmed before departure.

GOOD TO KNOW

Your questions,
answered.

Helpful information to plan your Calabar experience.

What is included in Seat Only?

A hospitality seat, meal, bottled water, non-alcoholic refreshment, check-in, seat guidance and event-day WhatsApp support. It excludes hotels, flights, airport pickup and attractions.

How much is Seat Only?

₦150,000 per person, subject to confirmation.

Can I add accommodation after buying a seat?

Yes, subject to hotel and room availability. A revised price will be quoted.

Which attractions are included?

Tinapa Free Zone & Resort and Kwa Falls — exactly two attractions in the standard Explore itinerary.

How long is the tour package?

Approximately three to five days. You can choose two, three or four hotel nights.

Does the package include flights?

Seat, Stay and Explore exclude flights. Fly & Explore includes a return flight subject to the departure airport, date, fare and availability.

Are airport transfers included?

Where specified in Fly & Explore and hosted international packages. Domestic Stay and Explore guests can add airport transfers.

Is the hotel named before payment?

A selected hotel class may appear initially. Final hotel names and room terms must be confirmed in writing before payment.

Can groups book?

Yes. Families, couples, companies, associations and diaspora groups can request coordinated seating, hotel rooms and transportation.

Is Davido confirmed to perform?

Davido's proposed star-artist billing is subject to final confirmation and approval by the event organisers.

What happens after booking?

Once your booking is genuinely confirmed, the team provides your booking reference, itinerary, guest guidance and relevant contact details.

Are prices refundable?

Refund terms depend on hospitality, hotel, flight and transport suppliers. Applicable cancellation rules should be confirmed before payment.
