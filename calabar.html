Tinapa Free Zone & Resort

A structured leisure and entertainment stop north of Calabar. Depending on operating status on your selected date, you may enjoy resort scenery, leisure facilities, food, retail and photography opportunities.

DESTINATION 02

Kwa Falls

A green nature escape inside the Cross River National Park area. Expect scenic surroundings, photography and an active walk to the falls. The approach includes substantial stairs and uneven terrain.

GOOD TO KNOW

Your questions,
answered.

Everything you should know before planning your Calabar experience.

What is included in Seat Only?

One hospitality seat, event-day meal, bottled water, non-alcoholic refreshment, guest check-in, guidance and event-day WhatsApp support. Hotels, flights, transfers and attractions are excluded.

How much is Seat Only?

₦150,000 per person, subject to confirmation of hospitality allocation.

Can I add accommodation after buying my seat?

Yes, subject to availability. A revised package quote will be calculated for your dates.

Which attractions are included?

The standard Explore route includes exactly two attractions: Tinapa Free Zone & Resort and Kwa Falls.

How long is the Explore package?

Approximately 3–5 days, with two, three or four hotel nights depending on your itinerary.

Does every package include flights?

No. Seat, Stay and Explore exclude flights. Fly & Explore includes a return flight subject to route, date and fare availability.

Are airport transfers included?

Where specified in Fly & Explore and hosted international packages. Domestic Stay and Explore guests may add shared or private transfers.

Will my hotel be named before payment?

A hotel class may initially be shown. Named hotel confirmation depends on availability and supplier terms, and should be provided before payment.

Can I book for a group?

Yes. Couples, families, organisations and diaspora groups can request coordinated rooms, seating, transport and guest support.

Is Davido confirmed to perform?

Davido's star-artist listing is subject to final event billing and approval. A live performance is not guaranteed here.

What happens after booking?

Once a genuine booking and payment are confirmed, guests should receive a booking reference, itinerary, pickup details, event instructions and support contacts.

Are prices refundable?

Cancellation and refund conditions depend on the seat, accommodation, airline and transport suppliers. All applicable terms must be shown before payment.
